I reproduced the Ecm unit mismatch on baseline commit `d74f56dd7f22925fb8148cabf5740ed8d1e39ff3` (macOS arm64, Python 3.12.16) and traced a related Excel export symptom.

**Calculation path.** `ConcreteProperties.Ecm` is documented in GPa (`dto.py:146`), and `PlateGirderBridge._build_material_props()` retains the database/custom value (33 for M40) in GPa (`plategirderbridge.py:1229-1238`). `PlateGirderBridge.design_deck_slab()` reads that value at `plategirderbridge.py:3186` and passes it unchanged at `:3193`. The callee specifies MPa (`deckdesign.py:429-430`); `_cracked_section()` divides `Es_MPa/Ecm_MPa` at `:211`. SLS stress, crack width and reinforcement sizing consume the affected ratio. Its results enter `output_dict["deck_design_results"]` and `output_dict["deck_report_values"]` at `plategirderbridge.py:3198-3201`. The girder/composite design path converts input Ecm by `* 1000` at `designer.py:336-337` and is separate.

Source permalinks at this commit: [GPa DTO](https://github.com/Nidhikhare12/OsdagBridge/blob/d74f56dd7f22925fb8148cabf5740ed8d1e39ff3/src/osdagbridge/core/bridge_types/plate_girder/dto.py#L146), [deck call](https://github.com/Nidhikhare12/OsdagBridge/blob/d74f56dd7f22925fb8148cabf5740ed8d1e39ff3/src/osdagbridge/core/bridge_types/plate_girder/plategirderbridge.py#L3186-L3193), [MPa cracked-section helper](https://github.com/Nidhikhare12/OsdagBridge/blob/d74f56dd7f22925fb8148cabf5740ed8d1e39ff3/src/osdagbridge/core/bridge_types/plate_girder/deckdesign.py#L205-L211), [girder conversion](https://github.com/Nidhikhare12/OsdagBridge/blob/d74f56dd7f22925fb8148cabf5740ed8d1e39ff3/src/osdagbridge/core/bridge_types/plate_girder/designer.py#L336-L337), [Excel resolver](https://github.com/Nidhikhare12/OsdagBridge/blob/d74f56dd7f22925fb8148cabf5740ed8d1e39ff3/src/osdagbridge/desktop/ui/dialogs/generate_results_values_builder.py#L215-L252).

**Isolated reproduction.** For `Es=200000 MPa`, `As=646 mm²/m`, `d=194 mm`: passing Ecm=33 to `_cracked_section()` yields α=6060.61 and neutral axis x=189.42 mm; converting 33 GPa to 33000 MPa yields raw α=6.06 and x=35.26 mm. At a synthetic 40 kNm/m service moment, `_sls_stress()` gives concrete stress 3.23 versus 12.45 MPa and steel stress 473.17 versus 339.75 MPa. These numbers are a fixed-section unit probe, not a full bridge rerun. Your issue already contains a full default-deck rerun.

**Excel impact.** The app-exported `Material Properties - Concrete` worksheet has Ecm heading “MPa” but E2=33 and “Modular Ratio” F2=6060.61; steel E is 200000 MPa. `generate_results_values_builder.py:215` reads GPa, `:217-224` divides MPa by it, and `:252` labels it MPa. `generate_results_dialog.py:1306-1384` writes the resolver values unchanged. This is a second source boundary, so fixing the deck call alone will leave the workbook wrong. The raw Es/Ecm value is **6.06** after conversion. IRC 22 applies a **minimum short-term design ratio of 7.5** in `irc22_2015.py:960-965`; the workbook currently displays a raw ratio, so 6.06 is its corrected value unless the field is relabelled as the code design ratio and explicitly applies the minimum.

**Proposed fixes.** Convert `self.material_props.concrete_prop.Ecm * 1000.0` before the `deckdesign.design_deck_slab()` call; retain the DTO/input dictionary's GPa contract. Convert `cp.Ecm * 1000.0` in `resolve_material_properties_concrete()` before assigning the MPa-labelled value and computing the exported raw ratio. Avoid rounding before the division.
